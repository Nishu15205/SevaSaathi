   688→
   689→    <p class="body-text">India is undergoing a profound demographic transformation. By 2026, the country will be home to over <strong>140 million elderly citizens</strong>, making it one of the fastest-aging populations in the developing world. Yet the infrastructure to support this shift remains dangerously inadequate. Unlike Western nations that built elder care systems over decades, India faces this challenge with fragmented services, no standardized quality benchmarks, and a workforce that is largely unorganized and unverified.</p>
   690→
   691→    <p class="body-text">Families seeking home care for aging parents or recovering patients confront a landscape of uncertainty. There is <strong>no trusted marketplace</strong> to find qualified caregivers. Most families rely on word-of-mouth referrals from neighbors or local agencies with zero accountability. When a caregiver underperforms, neglects medication schedules, or mistreats an elderly parent, there is no recourse, no review system, and no oversight body to turn to. The emotional toll on families already dealing with the stress of caregiving is immense.</p>
   692→
   693→    <p class="body-text">Pricing is equally opaque. Caregiver fees vary wildly between providers with no correlation to quality or experience. Families cannot compare options, verify credentials, or track the care their loved ones receive in real time. The entire ecosystem operates on <strong>trust without verification</strong>, and the consequences fall on the most vulnerable members of society. SevaSaathi exists to solve every one of these pain points with a single, comprehensive digital platform.</p>

   695→    <!-- SECTION 2: The Solution -->
   696→    <div class="chapter-header">
   697→        <div class="section-tag">Section 02</div>

   701→
   702→    <p class="body-text">SevaSaathi is a <strong>complete digital platform</strong> that connects families in need of home care with thoroughly verified, qualified caregivers. Unlike generic classifieds or unstructured referral networks, SevaSaathi provides an end-to-end experience: from creating a detailed patient profile with medical history and dietary requirements, through AI-powered smart matching that considers skill sets, location proximity, experience levels, and real-time availability, all the way to secure payment processing and post-care reviews.</p>
   703→
   704→    <p class="body-text">At its core, the platform features a <strong>real-time care monitoring system</strong> powered by Socket.io. Caregivers submit daily care reports documenting the patient's activities, mood, meals, and medication adherence. Families receive instant notifications and can monitor care quality as it happens, not days later. This transparency layer transforms home care from a blind trust exercise into a data-driven, accountable service delivery model.</p>
   705→
   706→    <p class="body-text">The platform is built on a modern tech stack featuring Next.js 16 with React 19, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS 4. It runs as a Progressive Web App for offline access, supports multiple authentication methods including Google OAuth and phone OTP, and integrates AI-powered document verification using the z-ai-web-dev-sdk for Aadhaar OCR processing. Every design decision prioritizes reliability, speed, and the specific needs of the Indian home care market.</p>

   916→        <div class="sec-dot"></div>

   924→        <div class="sec-dot"></div>
   925→        <div class="sec-text"><strong>JWT Session Management:</strong> Sessions use JSON Web Tokens with a 30-day expiry. Tokens are validated on every API request, ensuring that expired or tampered sessions are immediately rejected.</div>

   928→        <div class="sec-dot"></div>
